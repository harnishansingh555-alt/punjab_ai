How it works

Three steps and you are chatting.

  1. Sign in with Google

    One tap. No new password to remember.

  2. Ask in your language

    Type in English, Punjabi, or Punjabi in Roman letters.

  3. Read or listen

    Answers come back in Gurmukhi script and can be read aloud.

Questions

Do I need a Punjabi keyboard?

No. Type Punjabi in Roman letters, like “ki haal hai”, and Punjab AI replies in Gurmukhi. You can also use the Speak button.

What do you do with my Google account?

Only your name, email and photo are used, to show who is signed in. Your chats are saved in your own browser, not on a public server.

Can Punjab AI be wrong?

Yes. It can make mistakes, so check important information such as medical, legal or money matters.

Why is there no Punjabi voice on my device?

Reading Punjabi aloud needs a Punjabi voice installed on your phone or computer. Voice input works best in Chrome or Edge.
